# What Medical Procedures Are Most Popular for Medical Tourism in India?

Not every treatment travels equally well.

Some procedures are worth crossing continents for — because the savings are transformative, the waiting lists at home are punishing, or because India has built a depth of expertise that's genuinely hard to match elsewhere. Others are better handled locally.

This guide covers the procedures that consistently bring international patients to India, and what makes each one worth the journey.

## 1. Cardiac Surgery

**Why India:** Heart surgery is where medical tourism in India built its reputation. Centres like Narayana Health perform among the highest volumes of cardiac procedures in the world. High volume means deep expertise — surgeons who have seen and managed every variation, not just the textbook ones.

**What's available:** Coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG), valve repair and replacement, angioplasty and stenting, pacemaker implantation, congenital heart defect repair.

**The numbers:** A bypass surgery that costs $70,000–$200,000 in the US costs $4,000–$8,000 in India. [Compare your exact procedure →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Patients from the US, UK, Nigeria, Kenya, and the Middle East — often quoted months of waiting time at home, or costs that simply don't work.

## 2. Joint Replacement

**Why India:** Knee and hip replacement outcome quality correlates directly with how many procedures a team performs. India's leading orthopaedic centres perform hundreds per surgeon per year. The implants are identical to those used in Western hospitals — Stryker, Zimmer Biomet, DePuy Synthes. The cost is a fraction.

**What's available:** Total knee replacement, bilateral knee replacement, hip replacement, partial knee replacement, spinal fusion, shoulder replacement.

**The numbers:** A knee replacement costing $30,000–$70,000 in the US costs $4,000–$8,000 in India. Bilateral knee replacement — which many Western systems won't do simultaneously — is $7,000–$14,000 total. [Use the cost calculator to compare →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Patients in their 50s and 60s who've spent a year on NHS or Canadian waiting lists, or who face private costs that would wipe out savings.

## 3. Cancer Treatment

**Why India:** Two things drive oncology patients to India — cost and speed. Immunotherapy drugs that cost $10,000–$20,000 per cycle in the US cost $1,500–$5,000 here. And while a UK patient waits 4–8 weeks for their diagnostic pathway to complete before treatment begins, an oncology consultation in India can happen within days.

**What's available:** Surgical oncology (robotic and open), chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy, radiation (CyberKnife, Gamma Knife, IMRT), bone marrow transplant, PET-CT staging.

**The numbers:** A full chemotherapy course: $3,000–$15,000 in India versus $30,000–$100,000 in the US. Bone marrow transplant: $15,000–$35,000 versus $100,000–$300,000. [See the full comparison →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Patients who've exhausted insurance coverage, those seeking immunotherapy not covered at home, and patients on long diagnostic waiting lists for whom speed is medically critical.

## 4. IVF and Fertility Treatment

**Why India:** After two or three failed IVF cycles at home, the cumulative cost becomes unsustainable for most couples. A complete IVF cycle in India costs $2,000–$4,000 versus $15,000–$25,000 in the US or £5,000–£8,000 in the UK. Laboratory technology and embryologist training are comparable to leading Western clinics. Success rates at accredited centres are too.

**What's available:** IVF, ICSI, frozen embryo transfer, donor egg IVF, PGT-A genetic testing, sperm donation.

**The numbers:** Three IVF cycles in India — including travel and accommodation — often costs less than a single cycle at home. [Check your IVF cost comparison →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Couples who've had one or more failed cycles at home, those seeking donor egg IVF, and patients wanting a specialist second opinion on why previous cycles haven't worked. More detail in [Sano's IVF guide](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/app/pages/treatments.html).

## 5. Spine Surgery

**Why India:** Spine surgery is expensive, often delayed, and frequently means months of pain while waiting. India's leading neurosurgical centres perform high volumes of spinal procedures with navigation technology and robotic assistance where appropriate.

**What's available:** Spinal fusion, discectomy, laminectomy, vertebroplasty, scoliosis correction, artificial disc replacement.

**The numbers:** Spinal fusion costs $60,000–$110,000 in the US. In India: $5,000–$9,000. [Compare spine surgery costs →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Patients with degenerative disc disease, spinal stenosis, or scoliosis who've been waiting months at home or face costs their insurance won't cover.

## 6. Dental Treatment

**Why India:** Dental work is almost never covered by insurance anywhere — which makes every cost out of pocket, and Western out-of-pocket costs are extraordinary. A single implant costs $3,000–$6,000 in the US. In India: $400–$800.

**What's available:** Dental implants, full mouth reconstruction, veneers, crowns and bridges, root canals, orthodontics.

**The numbers:** Full mouth reconstruction: $30,000–$60,000 in the US; $3,000–$8,000 in India. [Run the numbers for your dental treatment →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Patients needing multiple implants, full arch reconstruction, or extensive cosmetic work where the Western total isn't viable.

## 7. Eye Surgery

**Why India:** India has extraordinary institutional depth in ophthalmology — driven by a large domestic patient base and decades of high-volume practice. The result is world-class expertise at a fraction of Western private costs.

**What's available:** Cataract surgery, LASIK and SMILE refractive surgery, glaucoma treatment, retinal surgery, corneal transplant.

**The numbers:** Cataract surgery (both eyes): $6,000–$12,000 in the US; $800–$1,500 in India. LASIK: $4,000–$6,000 in the US; $500–$1,200 in India. [Compare eye surgery costs →](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/#compare)

**Who comes:** Patients needing bilateral cataract surgery, those seeking LASIK at a cost their home country makes prohibitive, and patients with complex retinal conditions.

**What's available:** Panchakarma detox, Abhyanga, Shirodhara, post-surgical Ayurvedic rehabilitation, women's wellness retreats.
