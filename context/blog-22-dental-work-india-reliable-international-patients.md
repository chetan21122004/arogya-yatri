# Is Dental Work in India Reliable for International Patients?

Dental work is the procedure people are most sceptical about doing abroad — and the one where the savings are so large the scepticism is worth examining.

A single implant costs $3,000–$6,000 in the US. In India: $400–$800. Full mouth reconstruction: $30,000–$60,000 in the US; $3,000–$8,000 in India.

The savings are real. The question is whether the quality holds. Here's the honest answer.

---

## Is It Reliable?

At India's accredited dental clinics and hospital-based dental departments — yes.

Indian dentists complete a five-year BDS degree regulated by the Dental Council of India, with MDS specialisation for complex work. Many senior clinicians hold international postgraduate qualifications. The technology is the same: CBCT 3D scanning, CAD/CAM crowns, Nobel Biocare and Straumann implants.

What varies — as it does everywhere — is quality between clinics. A reputable accredited clinic is not the same as a walk-in dental shop in a tourist area. That distinction is everything.

---

## What Travels Well — and What Doesn't

**Good candidates for dental tourism:**
- Dental implants — single, multiple, or full arch (All-on-4, All-on-6)
- Full mouth reconstruction — crowns, bridges, veneers
- Root canals and standard restorative work

**Requires more planning:**
- **Full arch implants** — needs two trips: placement first, then final prosthetics 3–6 months later after osseointegration
- **Orthodontics** — ongoing adjustments over months; not suited to a single trip
- **Complex jaw surgery** — possible, but requires specialist planning and a longer stay

## What to Look for in a Clinic

**NABH accreditation or hospital affiliation** — the clearest quality signal. Independent oversight matters.

**CBCT 3D imaging** — mandatory for safe implant placement. Non-negotiable.

**International implant brands** — Nobel Biocare, Straumann, Osstem, Zimmer Biomet. Ask which system is used before you book.

**Itemised treatment plan before you travel** — a trustworthy clinic sends this based on your X-rays, not after you're in the chair.

**Experience with international patients** — ask directly. Clinics that regularly treat overseas patients have systems for remote follow-up. Those that don't will be improvising.

## Planning the Trip

Dental visits are shorter and lighter than surgical ones — most patients stay in a hotel rather than a hospital, and are back to gentle activity within a day or two.

The main thing to plan around is the two-trip timeline for implants: placement now, final prosthetics 3–6 months later. Many patients use the gap to visit India again — [Sano's destinations team](https://www.sanohealth.co.in/app/pages/destinations.html) can help plan both visits around your appointments.

Bring recent dental X-rays (OPG panoramic), your dental history, and a full medication list — especially anticoagulants or bisphosphonates, which affect treatment planning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will implants placed in India last as long?**
Longevity depends on the brand, technique, your bone density, and oral hygiene — not the country. International-brand implants placed by an experienced clinician carry the same life expectancy anywhere.

**What if my crown needs adjustment after I'm home?**
Any dentist can adjust it with your treatment records. Getting detailed documentation before you leave India is essential — shade, material, and specifications of every restoration.
