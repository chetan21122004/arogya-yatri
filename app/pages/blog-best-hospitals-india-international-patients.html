What Are the Best Hospitals in India for International Patients?

Most articles give you a ranked list of hospital names and stop there. This guide explains how to read hospital quality and what the selection process should actually look like.

It's the most searched question in medical tourism — and the one with the most unhelpful answers online.

Most articles give you a ranked list of hospital names and stop there. But a name tells you nothing about whether that hospital is right for your condition, your budget, your language, or your timeline.

This guide gives you something more useful: how to read hospital quality, which cities lead for which specialties, and what the selection process should actually look like.

What "Best" Actually Means

There is no single best hospital in India. There is the best hospital for your specific case.

A centre that performs 800 cardiac surgeries a year may have an average orthopaedics department. A hospital with outstanding oncology may have limited IVF facilities. The right question is never "which hospital is ranked #1?" — it's "which hospital has the deepest experience with exactly what I need?"

Two markers tell you the most:

NABH accreditation — India's national hospital accreditation standard. Independently audited. Covers infection control, medication safety, clinical protocols, patient rights, and more. This is the minimum you should accept.

JCI accreditation — Joint Commission International. The global benchmark used by insurers in the US, Europe, and the Middle East. Held by roughly 30 hospitals in India. If your home insurance requires it, this is the mark to look for.

Both are verifiable independently — NABH at nabh.co and JCI at jointcommissioninternational.org. Don't take a hospital's word for it.

Which Cities Lead for Which Specialties

India's medical tourism is concentrated in six cities. Each has strengths.

Chennai Consistently ranked India's top medical tourism destination. Strongest in cardiac surgery, oncology, orthopaedics, and organ transplant. Hospitals here treat an exceptionally high volume of international patients — particularly from Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia — and the international patient infrastructure reflects it.

Mumbai India's financial capital has some of its most sophisticated hospitals. Strong across all major specialties, with particular depth in oncology and neurology. Also has the most developed international patient services among India's cities.

Delhi (NCR) Strong in cardiac surgery, orthopaedics, and neurosurgery. Several of India's largest hospital groups have their flagship facilities here. High volume, high specialisation.

Hyderabad A growing medical hub with strong capabilities in cardiac care, fertility treatment, and ophthalmology. Generally lower costs than Mumbai or Delhi with comparable quality at the top end.

Bengaluru Strong in fertility and IVF, ophthalmology, and orthopaedics. A technology-forward city with well-equipped hospitals and a large English-speaking medical workforce.

Pune Home base of Sano Healthcare and Tourism. Strong in orthopaedics, cardiac care, and wellness. Increasingly popular as a medical tourism destination with lower costs than Mumbai and excellent connectivity.

The Hospital Groups International Patients Trust Most

Rather than ranking individual hospitals — which changes with leadership, accreditation cycles, and department-level variation — it's more useful to know the groups with consistently strong international patient records.

Apollo Hospitals — The largest private hospital network in Asia. Multiple JCI-accredited facilities. Strong international patient departments, particularly in Chennai and Delhi. Handles a high volume of overseas cases annually.

Fortis Healthcare — Major presence across Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru. JCI accreditation at several sites. Known particularly for cardiac and orthopaedic departments.

Manipal Hospitals — Strong in South India. Particularly well-regarded for oncology, transplant, and neurology.

Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital (Mumbai) — JCI-accredited. Known for high-complexity cases across specialties. Frequently cited for robotic surgery capabilities.

Narayana Health — Founded by cardiac surgeon Dr Devi Shetty, known globally for making high-quality cardiac surgery accessible. Exceptional cardiac volume and outcomes. Facilities in Bengaluru and Kolkata among others.

Ruby Hall Clinic (Pune) — One of Pune's leading multi-specialty hospitals. Strong in cardiac care and orthopaedics, with growing international patient services.

This is not an exhaustive list — India has hundreds of excellent accredited hospitals. The ones above are a starting point, not a ceiling.

What to Ask Before Choosing Any Hospital

A name and an accreditation certificate are not enough. These five questions separate genuinely excellent hospitals from adequately accredited ones:

How many cases of my specific procedure does this department perform per year? Volume is the strongest predictor of surgical outcomes. Ask for the number specific to your procedure — not the specialty broadly.

Who is the surgeon, and what is their individual case volume? The hospital's reputation means little if the surgeon assigned to your case is junior. Ask for the specific surgeon's experience.

Is there a dedicated international patient department with a named coordinator? Not a department in name only — an actual team that speaks your language, handles insurance paperwork, and stays with your case.

Can you provide an itemised cost estimate before I travel? Transparent pricing is a trust signal. Evasion on costs before travel often predicts surprises on the bill after.

What does post-treatment follow-up look like for patients returning home? The answer reveals how seriously the hospital treats international patient care versus international patient acquisition.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a hospital's accreditation?

NABH accreditation is searchable at nabh.co. JCI accreditation is verified at jointcommissioninternational.org. Don't rely on a hospital's own website claim — check the source directly.

Do I need a JCI-accredited hospital?

Not necessarily — many excellent hospitals hold NABH but not JCI accreditation. JCI matters if your home insurance specifically requires it for reimbursement. Ask your insurer before assuming.
